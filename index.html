AT A GLANCE

Beach access without leaving the complex.

Grand Caribbean is a smaller Gulf-front condominium community south of central Port Aransas. The property has a pedestrian boardwalk through the dunes to the beach.

Community amenities include a pool, grilling area, and outdoor shower. Individual condos are independently owned, so interiors, sleeping arrangements, managers, and booking terms vary by unit.
